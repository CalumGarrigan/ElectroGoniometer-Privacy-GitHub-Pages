1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the ElectroGoniometer Android application ("ElectroGoniometer", "the app"). ElectroGoniometer is a measurement aid that uses compatible Android device motion sensors to assist with measuring and recording joint or segment angles.

This policy describes the app's current data practices. If the app's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

2. Data the app accesses and processes

Motion and orientation sensor data

ElectroGoniometer accesses motion/orientation information available from the device, such as accelerometer or gravity-sensor readings, to calculate device orientation, movement angle, sensor stability, and out-of-plane tilt.

Measurement records

When you save a measurement, the app may store:

  • capture date and time;
  • collection mode;
  • joint, movement, body side, trial number, and phone orientation;
  • measured angle, signed angle, start orientation, and end orientation;
  • sensor stability, out-of-plane tilt, and sensor type.

User-entered study information

In Study Collection mode, you may enter a study identifier, participant identifier, and notes. These fields are user-controlled and may contain personal, research, or health-related information depending on what you enter.

Device and app information

Saved measurement records may include the device manufacturer and model, Android operating-system version, and ElectroGoniometer app version. The app also stores local preferences such as appearance, displayed precision, smoothing level, and default landscape orientation.

3. How the data is used

ElectroGoniometer uses the data described above to:

  • calculate and display live range-of-motion measurements;
  • show measurement-quality indicators such as stability and alignment;
  • save measurements for later review on the device;
  • display summaries and trends from saved measurements;
  • create CSV exports when you request them; and
  • remember local app settings.

The current app does not use measurement records for advertising, profiling, or automated marketing.

4. Local storage and network transmission

Saved measurement records are stored in ElectroGoniometer's private on-device database. App preferences are stored locally on the device.

The current production source does not request Android's INTERNET permission and does not automatically transmit measurement records, study identifiers, participant identifiers, notes, or sensor measurements to a developer-controlled server.

ElectroGoniometer does not provide its own cloud-sync service or user account system. Android device migration or device-transfer features may handle app data according to the operating system, device manufacturer, and your device settings.

5. Export and sharing

ElectroGoniometer can create CSV files containing selected saved measurements. Export is initiated by you.

You may save a CSV through Android's system document interface or share it through Android's system share sheet. When you choose a destination such as email, cloud storage, messaging, or another application, that destination may receive the exported data and will handle it under its own privacy practices.

For share-sheet exports, ElectroGoniometer uses Android's FileProvider mechanism to grant the receiving app temporary read access to the exported file.

Exported copies are outside ElectroGoniometer's private database. Deleting data inside ElectroGoniometer does not automatically delete copies that you previously saved or shared elsewhere.

6. Data retention and deletion

Saved measurements remain in the app's local database until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android device-transfer or system behavior described above.

ElectroGoniometer provides controls to delete individual measurements and a Delete all local data option in Settings.

Because ElectroGoniometer does not create user accounts or automatically upload saved measurements to a developer server, there is no remote account dataset for the developer to delete. Any CSV copies saved or shared outside the app must be deleted from those destinations separately.

7. Security and data handling

ElectroGoniometer uses Android app-private storage for its local measurement database. Data exported through the share sheet is exposed using a content URI with temporary read permission rather than by making the app's private database directly accessible.

No method of storing data on a device can guarantee absolute security. Users handling research, clinical, or identifiable participant information should also follow their organization's device-security, consent, retention, and data-governance requirements.

8. Third-party services, analytics, and advertising

The current ElectroGoniometer build does not contain third-party advertising, analytics, social-login, or developer-operated cloud-sync functionality.

The app uses standard Android and AndroidX software libraries to provide its user interface and Android functionality. If you intentionally export data to another app or service, that third party's privacy policy applies to the copy you send to it.

9. Children's privacy

ElectroGoniometer is not specifically designed for or directed toward children. The app does not knowingly operate a developer-controlled service that collects children's personal information.

If the app is used in research, education, clinical, or supervised settings involving minors, the responsible user or organization is responsible for obtaining any required consent and complying with applicable privacy, research, and child-protection requirements.

10. Research and health-related information

Joint-angle measurements, participant identifiers, and notes may constitute research, health, or other sensitive information depending on how ElectroGoniometer is used and what information is entered.

Where appropriate, users should use de-identified participant codes instead of names or other direct identifiers and should follow the data-management requirements applicable to their study, organization, or jurisdiction.

ElectroGoniometer is a measurement aid and is not represented as a validated medical diagnostic device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease or replace professional medical judgment.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be revised when ElectroGoniometer's functionality, data handling, legal requirements, or distribution practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page identifies the current published version.
